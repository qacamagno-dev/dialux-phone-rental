Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about renting with DiaLux.

How do I rent a device?
Browse available devices, select a daily, weekly, or monthly rental plan, add the device to your cart, then complete checkout and payment.
What payment methods are accepted?
DiaLux supports GCash, Maya, credit or debit card, Cash on Delivery, and bank transfer.
Is there a security deposit?
Yes. A refundable security deposit is required for rentals. The amount depends on the rental plan and will be reviewed after the device is returned and inspected.
How will I receive my device?
Available delivery options may include delivery, pickup, or meet-up depending on your order. You can track delivery updates from My Orders.
How do I return a rented device?
Open your order and request a return once the rental is eligible. A rider may be assigned for the return, then the device will be inspected by DiaLux.
When will my deposit be refunded?
The deposit is handled after the returned device has completed inspection. Any applicable charges or issues are reviewed before the rental is completed.
Can I change or reset my password?
Yes. You can change your password from Profile → Account Security. If you forgot it, use the password reset option and follow the link sent to your registered email.
Who can submit a device review?
Only customers who have actually rented the device can submit a rating and review.
What if I have an order or payment problem?
You can ask DiaLux Assistant above for quick help or submit a support ticket below. Include your order reference when available.
