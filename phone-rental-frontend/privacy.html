Privacy Policy

How we handle your data – with transparency and care.

Our Commitment

Your privacy is important to us. DiaLux collects minimal data and never sells or shares your personal information with third parties.

1. What We Collect

  • Account data: Name, email address, phone (optional), and delivery address.
  • Rental history: Devices rented, rental dates, and transaction details.
  • No payment data: All payments are processed through the payment gateway – we do not store credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process rental orders and payments.
  • To send order confirmations and rental reminders.
  • To improve our platform and customer support.

3. Secure Data Wiping

Every device returned to DiaLux undergoes a factory reset and secure data wipe before it is rented again. The wipe is logged by our technician team. Your personal data never stays on any device after return.

🔐 Data wiping is mandatory and logged. No device is relisted without a verified secure wipe.

4. Data Retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting support.

5. Security

We use industry‑standard encryption (JWT, bcrypt, HTTPS) to protect your data during transmission and storage.

6. Third‑Party Services

We use MongoDB Atlas (cloud database) and Google OAuth for authentication. These services are GDPR‑compliant and follow strict security practices.

7. Your Rights

  • Access your data at any time in your Profile.
  • Request correction or deletion by contacting support.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time.
