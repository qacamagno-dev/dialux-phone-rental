Help Center

Quick answers for common rental questions.

📱 How do I rent a device?

Choose a device from the catalog, add it to your cart, sign in, select Daily, Weekly, or Monthly rental, choose a start date, and complete checkout.

🔄 Can I extend a rental?

Yes. Active rentals have an "Extend" action in your My Rentals page. This adds 7 days to your current rental period.

📦 How do returns work?

Request a return from My Rentals. The device enters the technician queue for inspection, secure data wiping, and deposit‑refund processing.

🔐 What happens to my data?

Returned devices are factory‑reset and the wipe is logged before the device becomes available again. Your personal data is never stored on the device after return.

💰 What is the security deposit?

The security deposit is shown separately during checkout and is fully refundable after the returned device passes inspection.

🛡️ What does damage protection cover?

Optional protection can be added at checkout. It reduces the risk of unexpected repair charges during your rental. See our Terms & Conditions for details.
