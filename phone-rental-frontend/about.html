About DiaLux

Technology when you need it, without the burden of ownership.

Our Mission

DiaLux exists to make premium technology accessible to everyone – not just those who can afford to buy. We believe in access over ownership, giving students, professionals, and travellers the freedom to use the latest devices without the long‑term commitment.

Why DiaLux?

We combine trust, transparency, and flexibility with a premium experience. Every device is graded (Like New / Good / Fair), thoroughly tested, and data‑wiped before it reaches you. Our rental plans adapt to your schedule – daily, weekly, or monthly.

✨ Built by students, for the community. DiaLux is a capstone project for IT 009 – Systems Integration and Architecture at the Technological Institute of the Philippines – Quezon City.

Our Values

  • 🔐 Trust – Device grading, data wiping, and transparent policies.
  • ♻️ Sustainability – Extending device life through reuse and responsible maintenance.
  • 📱 Accessibility – Making premium tech affordable and flexible.
  • 💬 Support – 24/7 AI assistance and real‑human support.
